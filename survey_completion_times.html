What stands out

Participant 9's demographics page (781s / 13.0 min) is the same gap already flagged in the earlier timing report as likely idle time rather than genuine reading/typing time — no other participant spent more than 215s on demographics, and there's no blur/focus event around it (consistent with "tab stayed in focus, participant stepped away" rather than switching windows).
Participant 23's Work Habits page (364s / 6.1 min) is the single longest non-demographics survey time in the whole set — over 90s more than the next slowest (participant 7, 200s). Work Habits also includes an open-ended job-description field, so this could be genuine (a longer description) rather than idle; worth a quick look at their actual jobDescription text if you want to check.
KAI is consistently the slowest "quick" survey across nearly everyone (110-238s), which tracks with it being the longest instrument here (33 items) — Big Five (10 items) and Three Good Lives (15 items, but simple click-to-select) are the fastest for every participant.
